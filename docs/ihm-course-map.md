# Course map

How the academy modules cover the courses in five Swedish vocational (YH) data programs. The primary program is IHM Business School's **Customer Data Developer**; the others are mapped so the academy covers the whole field from data engineering to digital analytics.

## IHM · Customer Data Developer

| Course | Primary skills | Supporting skills | Bridge to the chain |
|---|---|---|---|
| Teknik för digitala plattformar (digital platform technology) | `ae-web-tracking` | `ae-project-method`, `ae-git-devops`, `ae-apis` | Where data is born: the click, the request, the cookie, the event |
| Datadrivna affärsprocesser och affärsutveckling (data-driven business processes) | `ae-business-strategy`, `ae-business-journeys` | `ae-customer-data-platforms`, `ae-governance-gdpr`, `ae-bi-metrics` | Purpose, business models, goals, KPIs, journeys, data sources and flows |
| Marketing Technology Data & Customer Data | `ae-martech`, `ae-crm-automation`, `ae-customer-data-platforms` | `ae-business-journeys`, `ae-web-tracking`, `ae-governance-gdpr` | The systems that use customer data and the KPIs they are judged on |
| Data Management & Data Design | `ae-modeling` | `ae-sql`, `ae-governance-gdpr`, `ae-dbt` | How data is structured so it can be trusted |
| Data Technology Stack | `ae-data-engineering`, `ae-warehouses` | `ae-dbt`, `ae-mcp-tooling` | The tools that move, store and transform data |
| API & Integrationsteknologi | `ae-apis` | `ae-systems-processes`, `ae-data-engineering`, `ae-python-oop` | How systems talk to each other |
| Objektorienterad programmering 1 & 2 | `ae-python-oop` | `ae-git-devops`, `ae-apis` | Structured, tested, reusable code |
| GDPR & Juridik | `ae-governance-gdpr` | `ae-web-tracking`, `ae-martech` | What you may do with data, and how to prove it |
| AI och maskininlärning inom Customer Data Development | `ae-ai-ml` | `ae-statistics`, `ae-analytics`, `ae-mcp-tooling` | Prediction, segmentation, LLMs and agents on customer data |
| Projekthantering & utvecklingsprocesser | `ae-project-method` | `ae-git-devops`, `ae-presentation-design` | How a team delivers |
| LIA (internship) | `ae-career` | all | Applying the chain at a real company |
| Examensarbete – Business Case | `ae-business-journeys`, `ae-business-strategy` | `ae-analytics`, `ae-presentation-design` | The capstone becomes the business case |

## Academic integrity

When an exercise or question belongs to graded coursework, the coach switches to LEARN mode: it explains concepts, works parallel examples, reviews your own attempt and debugs errors you show it, but it does not write the answer you hand in. You must always be able to explain anything AI helped you with.
