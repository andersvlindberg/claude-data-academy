# Learning log

One entry per session. Short is fine. The coach can fill it in with you at the end of a session.

```markdown
## YYYY-MM-DD · <module> · <topic>

**What:** what I worked on
**Why:** why it matters (link it to a business question or the chain)
**Result:** what I built or can now do
**Verification:** how I proved it works (test passed, row counts match, explained it back)
**Level:** L1 / L2 / L3 / L4
**Still unsure about:** open questions for next time
**Review on:** date for spaced repetition (+1 day, +1 week, +1 month)
```
