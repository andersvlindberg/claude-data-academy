# Frameworks and templates

## Business Model Canvas (with data lens)
| Block | Questions | Data it produces or needs |
|---|---|---|
| Customer segments | Who do we serve? | Customer data, segmentation |
| Value propositions | What problem do we solve? | Product data, reviews, surveys |
| Channels | How do we reach and deliver? | Channel and traffic data |
| Customer relationships | How do we keep customers? | CRM, loyalty, service data |
| Revenue streams | How do we earn money? | Transactions, pricing |
| Key resources | What do we need? | Inventory, staff, IP |
| Key activities | What must we do well? | Process and operations data |
| Key partners | Who helps us? | Supplier and partner data |
| Cost structure | What does it cost? | Cost and finance data |

## Goal tree worked example
- **Vision:** be one of the Nordic region's leading home furnishing e-retailers.
- **Business goal:** grow online revenue 20% in 2027 with stable margin.
- **Strategic goals:** grow returning customers; improve online conversion.
- **Tactical goals:** launch loyalty program in Q1; redesign product pages by Q2.
- **Operational goals:** weekly newsletter to segments; fix the top 10 checkout errors per month.
- **Activities:** build segments, A/B test product page, monitor checkout errors.
- **KPIs:** revenue (lagging), returning customer share (lagging), add-to-cart rate (leading), checkout error rate (leading).

## Data maturity self-assessment (score 1–4 each)
| Dimension | 1 Ad hoc | 4 Data-driven |
|---|---|---|
| Strategy | No data goals | Data supports strategy explicitly |
| Culture | Gut feeling | Evidence expected in decisions |
| Data | Silos, manual exports | Integrated, trusted, documented |
| Tools | Spreadsheets | Modern stack, self-service |
| Skills | Few people | Literacy across functions |
| Governance | Unclear ownership | Owners, definitions, quality, privacy |
| Use | Reports nobody reads | Decisions and automation run on data |
