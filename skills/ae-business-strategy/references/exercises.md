# Exercises: Business acumen and strategy

## Set A (practitioner)

1. Write purpose, business idea, vision and mission for a company of your choice and explain the difference.
2. Fill a Business Model Canvas for a streaming service, with the data lens column.
3. Rewrite three vague goals as SMART goals.
4. Classify: measure, metric or KPI? Leading or lagging?

## Set B (independent)

1. Compare two competitors' Business Model Canvases and explain how their data needs differ.
2. Build a goal tree from vision to activities for a real estate agency wanting more data-driven sales, with prioritised KPIs and KPI cards.
3. Assess a company's data maturity with the self-assessment and propose three steps to the next level.
4. Present a one-slide answer to: "What business value would better customer data create for us?"

## Quiz bank

1. Purpose versus business idea?
2. Name the nine Business Model Canvas blocks.
3. Strategic versus tactical versus operational goals?
4. What does each letter in SMART stand for?
5. Leading versus lagging indicator, with one example each?
6. What is HiPPO?

## Interview questions

1. "How do you make sure your data work supports the company strategy?"
2. "Which KPIs would you choose for our e-commerce, and why only those?"
3. "What does data-driven mean to you?"
