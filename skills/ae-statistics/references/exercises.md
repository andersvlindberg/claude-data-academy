# Exercises: Statistics

## Set A (practitioner)

1. Compute mean, median, SD and IQR of order values; explain why mean and median differ.
2. Explain a 95% confidence interval to a marketing manager in two sentences.
3. Control: 300/10 000 converted; variant: 345/10 000. Run the test and report effect, CI and p-value.
4. Identify the flaw: "We ran 20 A/B metrics and one was significant, so the variant works."

## Set B (independent)

1. Plan an experiment: baseline CR 2.5%, MDE 10% relative, α 0.05, power 0.8. Sample size per variant, duration at 8 000 sessions/day, SRM check plan.
2. Bootstrap the difference in median order value between two segments.
3. Fit a regression of weekly revenue on ad spend with month controls; explain why the coefficient may not be causal.
4. Design a 6-question customer survey, critique it for bias, and plan the analysis.

## Quiz bank

2. Why does precision grow with √n?
3. Which test for comparing AOV between two groups with skewed data?
4. What is SRM?
5. Why are Likert scales reported with medians?
