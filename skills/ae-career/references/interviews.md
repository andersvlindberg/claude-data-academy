# Interviews

## Mock interview protocol (coach)
1. Agree the format: role, length (default 30 min), mix of technical/case/behavioural.
2. One question at a time; wait for the answer.
3. Follow-up like an interviewer: "why?", "what if the data were 100× bigger?", "what would you do differently?"
4. After each answer: what was strong, what was missing, a better structure.
5. End: overall score per area (SQL, modeling, dbt, business, communication) and three things to practise.

## Question bank (across the chain)
**SQL:** WHERE vs HAVING · find duplicates and choose which to keep · rolling 7-day revenue · top 3 per category · why a query got slower.
**Modeling:** choose the grain · star schema vs OBT · SCD2 with snapshots · surrogate keys.
**dbt:** walk through your project · late-arriving data in incremental models · a test failed overnight · stopping definition drift.
**Warehouses:** controlling cost · access without PII exposure · BigQuery vs Snowflake.
**Tracking:** click to BigQuery · why GA4 and orders disagree · server-side without double counting.
**Business:** turn a vague request into requirements · ROAS 5, double the budget? · prioritise three requests.
**Analytics:** conversion dropped yesterday · trust an A/B result.
**GDPR:** deletion request in a warehouse · analysts without personal data.
**AI:** churn model value · AI agent risks · AI-ready data.
**Behavioural:** a project you're proud of · a mistake and what you learned · conflict in a team · learning something fast.

## STAR template
```
Situation: context in one or two sentences
Task: what you were responsible for
Action: what you did (I, not we), with tools and reasoning
Result: measurable outcome + what you learned
```

## 60-second pitch structure
Who I am and where I'm heading (one sentence) → my moat (background + bridge) → evidence (the capstone in one line, with a number) → what I'm looking for and why this company.
