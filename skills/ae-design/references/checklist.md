# Design interrogation checklist

**Message:** one-sentence takeaway per view? Most important thing most prominent? Point clear in five seconds? Anything there only because templates have it?

**Typography:** max two families? Clear size and weight contrast? Comfortable line length and height? No broken words or numbers split from units? Titles consistent?

**Colour:** one dominant, one accent? Accent only for what matters? Contrast passes for text and icons? Palette fits the topic? Works in dark mode (web)?

**Layout:** everything on a grid with consistent margins? Gaps from one spacing scale? Real white space? Layouts vary but stay coherent? Works at the real viewing size and on a phone?

**Signature:** one motif repeated? One memorable hero moment? Would a designer see intent, not a template?

**Data:** each chart answers one question in its title? Key series highlighted, rest muted? Direct labels, honest axes, units?

**Polish:** no overflow, clipping or overlap in the rendered output? No placeholder text? Icons from one family? Microcopy tight and consistently capitalised? Sources cited?
