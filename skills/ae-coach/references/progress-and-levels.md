# Progress and levels

## Assessing a level

| Level | Ask | Pass when |
|---|---|---|
| L1 Aware | "Explain X in your own words and why it exists." | Correct core idea, no major misconception |
| L2 Practitioner | Give a standard task (exercise set A) | Solved with at most light hints |
| L3 Independent | Give an unfamiliar task (exercise set B) including debugging | Solved and verified without hints |
| L4 Interview-ready | Interview question with two follow-ups and a trade-off | Defends choices, names alternatives and their costs |

Never award a level based on the learner saying they understand. Award it on evidence.

## Log entry format

```markdown
## YYYY-MM-DD · <module> · <topic>
**What:**
**Why:**
**Result:**
**Verification:**
**Level:**
**Still unsure about:**
**Review on:**
```

Write it in the learner's language. Keep each line short. "Verification" must be concrete: "dbt test passed on 4 tests", "row count 1 204 matches source", "explained window frames back correctly".

## Where to keep it

- In the capstone repo: `docs/learning-log.md` (public, shows progression to recruiters, no personal data).
- Or in the learner's notes app.
